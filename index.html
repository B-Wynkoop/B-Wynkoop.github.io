About Me

I’m a junior cybersecurity professional with a strong foundation in security fundamentals, operating systems, networking, and system security. I’ve built my knowledge through experience with virtual machines, network environments, security tools, and practical labs, including extensive work through TryHackMe.

My interests span both offensive and defensive security, with a particular focus on threat detection and analysis, incident response, as well as penetration testing. I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying weaknesses, and thinking through how those weaknesses can be detected, exploited, and mitigated.

Whether I’m analyzing logs, troubleshooting a Linux system, configuring a virtual environment, investigating a security event,
or working through a challenging lab, I like to understand what is happening and why.
That mindset has helped me develop a practical understanding of cybersecurity rather than relying solely on theory.

Outside of cybersecurity, I enjoy golf, spending time outdoors, going to the beach, and spending time with family.
Maintaining these interests outside of technology has helped me continuosly stay balanced, focused, and motivated.
