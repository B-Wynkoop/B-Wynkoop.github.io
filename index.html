About Me

Curiosity is what first drew me to cybersecurity, but solving problems
is what keeps me in it. I enjoy understanding how systems and networks work,
identifying weaknesses, and figuring out how to make them more secure.
My knowledge spans networking, Linux, security operations, system hardening, and vulnerability
assessment, as well as constantly pushing myself to understand the technology at a deeper level.

Outside of tech I enjoy golfing, relaxing at the beach, and spending time with friends and family.
