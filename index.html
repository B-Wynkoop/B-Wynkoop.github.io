About Me

Cybersecurity is a field that naturally works with the way I think.
I enjoy understanding how technology works, digging into why something
behaves the way it does, as well as looking at where and how it can be improved.

What interests me most is the investigative side of cybersecurity.
I like taking something unfamiliar, breaking it down, and figuring out
what’s happening beneath the surface. Whether I’m working with Linux, analyzing
a network, investigating a vulnerability, or exploring how an attack works,
I’m always interested in understanding the bigger picture.

I’ve built a strong understanding across a variety of security operations
through Google courses, TryHackMe labs, projects, and continuously
challenging myself with new areas in the field.
