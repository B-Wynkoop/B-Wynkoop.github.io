About Me

Cybersecurity is a field that naturally works with the way I think.
I enjoy understanding how technology works, digging into why something
behaves the way it does, as well as looking at where and how it can be improved.

What interests me most is the investigative side of cybersecurity.
I like taking something unfamiliar, breaking it down, and figuring out
what’s happening beneath the surface. Whether I’m working with Linux, analyzing
a network, investigating a vulnerability, or exploring how an attack works,
I’m always interested in understanding the bigger picture.

Over time, I’ve built a strong foundation across networking, Linux,
security fundamentals, system hardening, vulnerability assessment,
and security operations. I’ve developed that knowledge through certifications,
technical labs, virtual environments, and continuously
challenging myself with new areas of the field.

I take the same approach to cybersecurity that I do to anything I care about:
stay curious, ask questions, and understand the details. There’s always something
new to learn in this field, and that’s one of the things I enjoy most about it.

Mission

My long term mission is to discover a vulnerability worthy of
its own CVE and contribute to having it publicly documented.
For me, that would represent more than just technical acheivement.
